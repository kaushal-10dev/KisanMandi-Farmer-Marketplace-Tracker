Problem

Farmers may lack timely and comparable market-price information and can face barriers reaching verified buyers directly.

Proposed direction

Market-price visibility, 7-day forecast display, market comparison, buyer/FPO discovery, alerts, and multilingual voice support.

Validation needed

Confirm official data-feed access, freshness, forecast quality, transport-cost availability, buyer verification and language accuracy before real-world deployment.
